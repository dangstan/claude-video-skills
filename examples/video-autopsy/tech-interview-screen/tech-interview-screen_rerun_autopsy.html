The re-run corrected the instruments, not the call

A second pass over an already-autopsied screening call, run to exercise rules added to the skill the same day they were written. Every correction below is to how the first pass measured, not to what happened in the recording -- and one of the new rules was refuted by the only recording available to test it.

Sanitized example. Real output of a real run. External-record sections were removed whole, identifiers scrubbed, quasi-identifiers coarsened. Coverage is deliberately rounded: this document originally published its measured window as a seconds pair whose denominator was the recording's exact length -- so the coarsened duration above was being contradicted three sections below it by arithmetic, and the gate missed it because it was scanning for the duration written as a duration. Dropping the denominator alone would not have been enough either: the numerator divided by an exact percentage recovers it to within a second. Coverage therefore ships as the window in mm:ss plus a percentage rounded to the nearest five. (The markdown's version of this note itself quoted the original seconds pair verbatim until the gate refused its own explanation -- a tombstone that repeats the removed value re-publishes it.)

Disposition
earlier framing overstated; consequence narrowed
The language= parameter did not force English output: "Entao, como eu disse, eu to actively looking, ne?" came through intact. The engine that actually failed was the speaker-separated export, whose recogniser is language-locked and which returned word salad plus no speaker labels for the same span. So metrics keyed to the EXPORT (talk-share, turn counts, disfluency) are void beyond 25:52 -- but whisper-derived pace and silence are not, which is a narrower consequence than the first pass claimed.

Section 3Screen and technical findings

  • The recording is a full-desktop OBS capture of the candidate's own machine, not a screen share -- the recruiter could not see any of it. Browser chrome, tab strip and extension bar are legible in every frame at 5 fps.
  • Google Meet's own live captions are on, legible and speaker-attributed throughout: a third transcript source, used here to confirm the face map.
  • The caption panel labels the candidate's own speech "You". That is a PERSONALISED label -- it identifies the account that made the recording, not whoever is speaking. It agreed with the active-speaker border and the export line at the checked timestamp, so it corroborated the map rather than establishing it.

Section 5Limitations

  • Export-derived metrics are void beyond 25:52 -- but whisper-derived pace and silence are not, which is a change from the earlier pass.
  • Attribution across the Portuguese span is inferred from turn logic, because the export supplies no speaker labels there. It is not measured.
  • The audio-capture check was not re-measured this run; the earlier measurement is carried.
  • The frame pass was targeted, not exhaustive: two frames, at 02:00 and 24:00, chosen to bind and re-verify the face map. The earlier pass's expression bursts were not repeated.
  • The outcome estimate, behavioural check and claims ledger were not re-derived; they are carried from the earlier pass and marked as such.
